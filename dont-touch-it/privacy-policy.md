# Privacy Policy - Don't Touch It: Tricky Puzzle

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game "Don't Touch It" (the "App"), published by Veera Reddy S ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

## Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.

Game progress and settings (such as unlocked levels and sound preferences) are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect your device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system version and language, and ad interactions (impressions and clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent tools.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with anyone other than the advertising provider described above.

## Data security and retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
