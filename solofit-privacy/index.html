Privacy Policy – SoloFit

App: SoloFit — Offline RPG Fitness (com.solofit.app)
Developer: Veera Reddy S
Effective date: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SoloFit mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. SoloFit is an offline fitness app that turns workouts into a levelling RPG. It requires no account and has no cloud server.

1. Information stored on your device

Your profile, level, stats, quests, achievements, workout history and GPS run records are stored only in a local database on your device. We do not operate servers that receive or store this information.

2. Camera (rep counting)

When you use camera-verified workouts, the App uses your camera to count repetitions and check form with on-device computer vision. Camera frames are processed in memory on your device in real time. They are never recorded, saved, or uploaded, and we never receive them. You can deny camera permission and still use the rest of the App.

3. Location (GPS runs and walks)

When you start a GPS run or walk, the App uses your device's precise location to measure distance, pace, and route. Location is processed and stored only on your device and is never sent to us or any server by the App. While tracking is active, the App runs a foreground service with a visible notification so that tracking continues when the screen is off. Location is used only during an active session you start, and you can deny location permission and still use the rest of the App.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interaction data, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data

This information is processed by Google under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Data retention and deletion

All of your fitness data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. We do not keep copies, so deleted data cannot be recovered.

6. Health disclaimer

SoloFit is for general fitness and motivation only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a doctor before starting a new exercise programme.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
